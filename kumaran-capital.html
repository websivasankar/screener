How We Work

The Kumaran Capital Process

A transparent, step-by-step approach tailored to your unique situation — no templates, no shortcuts.

1

Discovery

A one-on-one conversation to understand your current financial position, life goals, income, expenses, risk tolerance, and what financial independence means to you personally.

2

Analysis

Deep-dive into your cash flow, existing investments, insurance coverage gaps, liabilities, and tax situation — to build a clear picture of where you stand today.

3

Strategy

Planning exactly where and how to invest — asset allocation, fund selection, SIP amounts, insurance products — all mapped to your specific milestones and timeline.

4

Security

Identifying financial risks — market, health, income — and implementing the right safeguards so unexpected events don't derail your long-term financial plan.

5

Execution & Review

A clear, personalised action plan with ongoing reviews and rebalancing — because your financial plan should evolve as your life does.
